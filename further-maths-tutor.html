The course

What we cover

At GCSE level, Further Maths is usually the AQA Level 2 Certificate in Further Maths (two papers) or OCR Additional Maths. Both introduce A-level ideas such as calculus and matrices, and make the step up to A-level much easier. Read our free GCSE Further Maths guide.

At A-level, everyone studies core pure (complex numbers, matrices, proof by induction, further calculus and more), plus options chosen by your school. Read our free A-level Further Maths guide.

  • GCSE Further Maths and Additional Maths, alongside GCSE Maths
  • Core pure: complex numbers, matrices, induction, series, further calculus, polar coordinates and differential equations
  • Further statistics, further mechanics and decision maths options
  • Past papers and exam technique, marked with feedback

Yes. At GCSE level we teach the AQA Level 2 Certificate in Further Maths and OCR Additional Maths, and at A-level we teach Further Maths on Edexcel, AQA, OCR A and OCR MEI.

How much does Further Maths tutoring cost?

£25 an hour for GCSE Further Maths and £35 an hour for A-level Further Maths, paid per lesson with no long-term contract. The first session is free.

Can one tutor help with A-level Maths and Further Maths?

Yes. Most Further Maths students take both, and it helps to have one tutor who can see the whole picture.

Which options do you cover?

All of the common ones: further statistics, further mechanics and decision maths. Tell us your board and options when you book.
