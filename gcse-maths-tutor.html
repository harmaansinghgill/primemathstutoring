The course

What the exam looks like

Every board sets three papers of 1 hour 30 minutes, all sat at the end of Year 11, and each paper is worth a third of the grade. One is non-calculator. Foundation tier covers grades 1 to 5 and Higher covers grades 4 to 9. A formulae sheet is given in the exams up to 2027.

Read more in our free GCSE Maths guide.

  • Number, algebra, ratio and proportion, geometry, probability and statistics
  • Foundation and Higher, including moving up a tier
  • Exam technique and timing, paper by paper
  • Mock and past paper practice, marked with feedback
  • Grade 4 and 5 boundary work, and stretch towards 7, 8 and 9

Which exam boards do you cover for GCSE Maths?

AQA (8300), Edexcel (1MA1) and OCR (J560), at Foundation and Higher. If you're not sure which board your school uses, we'll help you find out on the free intro.

Can you help if my child is between Foundation and Higher?

Yes. We look at where they are now and what grade they're aiming for, and help you and their school decide. Lessons then focus on the topics that move them up.

How much does GCSE Maths tutoring cost?

£25 an hour, paid per lesson with no long-term contract. GCSE Further Maths is the same price. The first session is a free 20 to 30 minute call.

When should we start?

Year 10 gives the most time, but plenty of students start in Year 11 before the mocks. Lessons are planned around how many weeks are left.
