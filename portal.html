Adding a new student

  1. In Supabase, open Authentication, then Users, then Add user, then Create new user.
  2. Enter their email and a temporary password, tick Auto Confirm User, and create them.
  3. Refresh this page. They'll appear below: add their name and subjects, then save.
  4. Give them the temporary password. They can change it with the Password button once they've logged in.
