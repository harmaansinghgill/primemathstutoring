The course

What the exam looks like

Every board sets three papers, all sat at the end of Year 13. About two thirds of the content is pure maths and one third is applied, split between statistics and mechanics. On Edexcel, AQA and OCR A, each paper is 2 hours and worth 100 marks. Each board also has a large data set for statistics.

Read more in our free A-level Maths guide.

  • Pure: algebra, functions, trigonometry, calculus, sequences, vectors and proof
  • Statistics: data, probability, distributions and hypothesis testing
  • Mechanics: kinematics, forces, Newton's laws and moments
  • Your board's large data set
  • Past papers and exam technique, marked with feedback
